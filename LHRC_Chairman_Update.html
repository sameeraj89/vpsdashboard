6. Kerala Expansion — Considerations
  • We are evaluating an acquisition offer from Ernakulam Medical Centre (EMC) in Kochi.
  • The asset — an established, profitable ~300-bed tertiary hospital on the NH-66 Bypass, Palarivattom, minutes from Lakeshore; ~₹100 Cr revenue, a clean balance sheet (net debt ~₹3.5 Cr), 100% held by ~12 family promoters.
  • Current margins are low, creating an opportunity for VPS to lift EBITDA from ~6% to 15–20% (in line with tertiary peers) — entry risk is low, and the VPS model is built to close that gap via group procurement, case-mix upgrade and shared services.
  • Strategic fit — a second Kochi campus densifies the network: routine and secondary work runs at EMC while complex tertiary/quaternary cases route to the main Lakeshore campus, keeping high-value revenue inside VPS.
  • Indicative deal — while the ask is closer to ₹400 Cr, our initial estimate is that the EV is closer to ~₹150 Cr; this includes an unrealised real-estate value of about ₹75–100 Cr. We are still evaluating this and it is currently at a due-diligence stage.
  • Key risks — understanding the bottlenecks behind the low margin, and possible cannibalisation of the VPS Lakeshore catchment.

7. Jaipur / Kota — Indorama Proposal
  • Proposed partnership with the Indorama Group for two hospital centres in Rajasthan — Jaipur and Kota.
  • Structure — VPS takes 25% equity in each centre, contributed as medical equipment (~₹100 Cr, ~25% of the ~₹400 Cr project cost); one SPV per centre, 75% Indorama : 25% VPS.
  • Delivery & operations — VPS acts as turnkey project manager (PMC) from design through commissioning to NABH-ready handover, then operates both centres under a 10-year O&M agreement at 5% of net revenue plus 12.5% of EBITDA once profitable, with bulk procurement (~12–15% consumables saving) and co-branding "In clinical partnership with VPS Lakeshore."
  • Economics (5-yr base case) — combined net revenue ramps to ~₹160 Cr by Year 5; VPS recurring cash cumulates to ~₹43 Cr; blended 5-year IRR ~15% (including an equity exit at 8× post-O&M EBITDA).

Strategic Note
Project Nila — Assembling Kerala's Regional-Champion Network
VPS today runs a single sub-scale Kerala asset in Lakeshore Kochi, in a ₹35,000 Cr market entering its most significant ownership transition. A succession-stress wave (1970s–90s founders ageing out), an NCLT/CIRP distressed pipeline (SUT Royal in liquidation; capacity at a 40–60% discount to replacement cost), and a mission-hospital capital crunch (Lourdes, Caritas, Bishop Benziger seeking a brand-neutral operating partner) have opened a 24–36 month window. Project Nila assembles a ~8,300-bed, multi-district platform that multiplies specialist utilisation at the Lakeshore quaternary hub and captures the Gulf–NRI referral pipeline.
The two-playbook structure is the differentiator. Strategic-Control (17 hospitals, ~4,900 beds, ~₹2,300 Cr) consolidates economics through equity, NCLT resolutions and academic JVs. O&M concession (8 hospitals, ~3,360 beds, ~₹520 Cr capex only) lets VPS operate mission and trust hospitals — constitutionally barred from selling equity — for a 6.5% management fee plus EBITDA-share, with no acquisition cost and no real-estate. The financial model gives a base case of ~₹3,900 Cr deployed for ~20% equity IRR / 2.4× MOIC over five years (bear ~1.4×, bull ~3.5×). The immediate ask is approval-in-principle and authorisation to run the 60-day diligence track.
